My spin on a classic Chinese dish.

{{< figure src="/img/corn-soup.jpg" alt="A bowl of Chinese corn soup" width="50%" >}}

### Ingredients
- White pepper, to taste
- 1 tsp soy sauce, plus more to taste
- Sesame oil, to taste
- Green onions, for garnish
- Stock concentrate or bouillon for 500 mL water
- 2 eggs
- Silken tofu, to taste
- 500 g sweet corn
- 1 tbsp cornstarch

### Instructions
- **Create Soup Base:** Bring 500 mL of water to a boil and add the stock concentrate or bouillon according to its instructions.
- **Thicken:** In a separate bowl, mix 2 tbsp water with 1 tbsp cornstarch. Add this slurry to the soup and stir; it should thicken slightly.
- **Reduce heat to a simmer:** Do it, then add 1 tsp soy sauce.
- **Cook Eggs**: In a separate bowl, beat two eggs. Once frothy, very slowly drizzle the eggs into the broth; they should form thin strands.
- **Blitz Corn**: Blitz one-quarter of the corn with a splash of water or liquid from the canned corn.
- **Add Corn**: Combine all the corn and add it to the soup.
- **Add Tofu**: Rip up your tofu and add an amount you prefer. For higher protein, obviously add more tofu.
- **Garnish**: Chop the green onions and add them to the soup with white pepper, sesame oil, and more soy sauce to taste.
- **Serve to bai**: We did it boys.
