### Ingredients (Aromatics)
- 3 Garlic Cloves
- 1-inch piece of ginger

### Ingredients (Spice Mix)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp smoked paprika

### Ingredients
* Olive oil
- 1 Medium Yellow onion
- 500mL (14oz) coconut milk
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup dried red lentils (split okay)
- 100g of kale
- Salt and Black pepper
- Cilantro
- 1 lime

### Instructions
- **Cook Onions:** Oil the pan and drop in diced onions. Cook for 5-10mins, until soft.
- **Cook Aromatics:** Push onions over and drop in minced ginger/garlic, cook until fragrant. ~2mins
- **Activate all Spices:** Push everything over and, drop in entire spice mix add a little water or oil to help activate.
- **Add the rest**: Mix everything, then add coconut milk, veggie broth, lentils. Cook lentils fully.
- **Add Kale**: Reduce heat, add the kale cook for another minute. Don't overcook the Kale. Season with salt.
- **Serve**: Add to bowl, add black pepper, add chopped cilantro and finish with lime.

Enjoy.
