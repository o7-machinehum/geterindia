## Cantonese Chow Mein 

### Ingredients

#### Noodles & Vegetables
- 12 oz fresh chow mein egg noodles (Hong Kong–style, thin)
- ½ small yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 5 stalks green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces (whites and greens separated)
- 2 cups bean sprouts
- 3 tbsp neutral oil (corn, peanut, canola)
- 1 tsp sesame oil (finish)
- 200 g firm or extra-firm tofu, cut into thin slabs or cubes

#### Stir-Fry Sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp regular soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp of ginger
- ½ tsp fish sauce  
- 1 tbsp sugar  
- 2 tbsp water  

---

### Instructions

#### 1. Prep the Noodles
- Pour boiling water over fresh noodles and soak for **30 seconds–1 minute** until loosened.
- Drain. 

#### 2. Make the Sauce
- Mix all stir-fry sauce ingredients until sugar dissolves.

#### 3. Cook the Tofu
- Heat 1 tsp oil in a hot wok or skillet.
- Add tofu, lightly salt, and pan-fry until golden on both sides.
- Remove and set aside.

#### 4. Sauté Aromatics
- Heat 1 tbsp oil over medium-high heat.
- Add onion and **white parts of green onions**.
- Stir-fry for 1 minute, then remove.

#### 5. Pan-Fry the Noodles
- Add 1 tbsp oil to the hot wok.
- Spread noodles in a thin layer.
- Cook undisturbed for ~2 minutes until crispy.
- Flip, drizzle another 1 tbsp oil around the pan, cook another 2 minutes.

#### 6. Combine
- Add onions, green onion tops, bean sprouts, tofu, and sauce.
- Toss gently for 1–2 minutes until coated.
- Remove from heat, drizzle sesame oil, toss once more.

#### 7. Serve Immediately

---

### Notes
- Use **high heat** for proper noodle crisping.
- Always prep ingredients first (mise en place).
- If adding other proteins, cook them first and return at the end.

Serve with congee, gai lan, or simple steamed greens.
