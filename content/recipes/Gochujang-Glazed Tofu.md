### Ingredients (Sauce)
* 1-2 tbsp gochujang 
* 1 tsp miso paste
* 1 tsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp maple syrup or honey 
* 1 tbsp rice vinegar
* 1 tbsp sesame oil
* 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 tsp grated ginger
* Green onion (whites)

### Ingredients
* 1 block of firm tofu, cut into cubes
* Sticky Rice
* Grated carrots or cabbage
* Sliced cucumber
* Lettuce mix
* Acocado
* Kimchi
* Green onions

### Instructions
  - **Prepare the Tofu:** Press the tofu for about 15-20 minutes to remove excess water, helping it absorb the glaze better.
  - **Make the Glaze:** In a small bowl, mix the sauce ingredients and add 1/4 cup of hot water to bring together.  
  - **Pan-Fry the Tofu:** Heat vegetable oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu cubes and cook until golden brown on all sides.
  - **Glaze the Tofu:** Reduce the heat to low, drain the oil. Pour the glaze over the tofu and gently toss to coat, allowing the glaze to caramelize slightly on the tofu, about 1-2 minutes.
  - **Serve:** Remove from heat, assemble bowls, topped with green onions or sesame seeds if desired.
